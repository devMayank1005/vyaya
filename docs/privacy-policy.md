# Vyaya privacy policy

**Approved 2026-11-26.** Effective: <date of the first beta>. Fill in the contact email before publishing.

Vyaya is an expense tracker that works entirely on your phone.

## What Vyaya reads
- **Notifications** from UPI and bank apps (for example PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm and your bank's app), and from
  your SMS app. Vyaya reads them only after you allow notification access, and only to find payments. Notifications
  from any other app are ignored without being read.
- **SMS** (only in the version installed outside the Play Store): bank messages as they arrive, and the last 30
  days when you ask for an import.
- What you type in: payments, categories, budgets, recurring bills, people you split bills with.

## What Vyaya keeps, and where
Everything stays in the app's private storage on your phone:
- the payments found, with their amount, date, payee and category;
- your categories, accounts, budgets, rules and the people you split with.

The text of messages Vyaya skips is never kept. It keeps only a summary (amount, sender, date) for 30 days in the
Ignored tab.

## What leaves your phone
**Nothing**, unless you send it yourself:
- Vyaya has no internet permission. It can't send anything anywhere: no account, no servers, no analytics, no ads,
  no crash reporting.
- Android's automatic backup and device-to-device transfer are turned off for Vyaya.
- A **backup file** or **spreadsheet** you export is saved where you choose. A backup can be protected with a
  password (AES-256-GCM). Who you share these files with is up to you.

## Security
- An optional app lock asks for your fingerprint, face or the phone's PIN.
- While it is on, the app's screen is hidden from screenshots and the recent-apps preview. Budget alerts and the
  home-screen widget then show no amounts.

## Your choices
- Notification access, SMS access and notifications can be turned off at any time in your phone's Settings.
- Uninstalling Vyaya deletes everything it kept. Export a backup first if you want to keep it.

## Children
Vyaya is meant for adults managing their own money.
