## Findings

1. **[DATA-001] [High] Real bank SMS may name a payee differently from the app, and would now count twice**
   - Where: `Capture.payeesConflict` (SIM-006). An app notification and a bank SMS of the same amount within minutes
     are no longer one payment when both name a payee and neither name starts with the other.
   - Why: the synthetic messages name every payee the same way in every channel. Real ones may not:
     - a card SMS can carry the merchant's legal name ("BUNDL TECHNOLOGIES" for Swiggy);
     - a UPI SMS can carry a VPA that isn't the brand ("paytmqr2810050501011abcd@paytm").
     Then PhonePe's "Swiggy" and the bank's "BUNDL TECHNOLOGIES" become two entries.
   - Why it matters: one payment counted twice is worse than the rare same-amount merge it fixes (27 of 138,346 in
     the simulation).
   - Direction: use the payee only to *choose* among several same-amount candidates (prefer the one whose payee
     agrees). With a single candidate, merge as before. Check against your real samples before the beta.

2. **[FLAW-002] [Medium] A new phone usually captures payments before the restore, so the merge keeps the default settings anyway**
   - Where: `Restore.takesSettings` needs "nothing logged".
   - What happens: in the usual order the user installs, allows notification access (payments start arriving), and
     restores later. The captured entries count as "logged", so FLAW-008 still happens in the common case.
   - Direction: when the backup's settings differ from this phone's and this phone's are still the defaults, ask:
     "Use the backup's month start (25th) and time zone (New York)?". Or count only entries typed in.

3. **[FLAW-003] [Low] A damaged row shows on Home only once something reads it**
   - Where: damage is reported by the read that meets it. A damaged entry from last year shows only when that month
     is opened, or at export.
   - Direction: a light integrity pass at app open (records always; entries a page at a time, in the background), or
     accept. Export already checks everything first.

4. **[FLAW-004] [Low] A damaged settings row silently falls back to the defaults**
   - Where: `SnapshotStore.readable`. Home's card says "1 record couldn't be read" but not that it's the settings, so
     month boundaries change without a word.
   - Direction: name it on the card ("Your settings couldn't be read: the month now starts on the 1st").

5. **[PEN-001] [Low] Restore now cleans text silently**
   - Where: the restore reader applies `cleanText()`. A crafted backup is made harmless, but nothing says that text
     was changed.
   - Direction: count what was cleaned and say "N names or notes had hidden characters removed" after the restore.

6. **[FLAW-005] [Low] The header label rule allows no brackets**
   - Where: FLAW-011 allows letters, digits, `.`, `_`, `-`, `+` and spaces. A future `versionName` like
     "1.2 (beta)" would make the app refuse its own backups. After the first release that is a rule 8.7 change.
   - Direction: a test that the app's own `versionName` passes the rule, or allow brackets now.

7. **[SIM-001] [Info] Dependency updates now need a checksum step**
   - Every version bump needs `--write-verification-metadata sha256`, from an empty cache and on both macOS and Linux
     (aapt2 and Kotlin/Native have per-OS downloads), or CI fails.
   - Direction: a short "updating dependencies" section in the launch checklist, or a manual CI job that writes the
     metadata on Linux and uploads it as an artifact.

8. **[Info] Things that held up**
   - Damaged rows: Home still opens; export writes the readable rest; merge repairs a damaged entry from the backup.
   - The cross-feature reference is independent of the engine, and it catches a planted bug.
   - Text cleaning keeps Indian scripts' zero-width joiners, combining marks and emoji (flags arrive as surrogate pairs
     and stay).
   - Capture's transfers never set a destination account, so the new "transfer to the same account" rule can't make
     capture throw.
   - `CatalogScreenTest` failed once in the full gate and passed on reruns: an intermittent scroll timing in
     Robolectric. Noted, not changed.
