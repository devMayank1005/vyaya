# Findings: budgets (monthly limit per spending category): 2026-11-12

**Scope:** `253c615`, which covers:
- `Budgets` (core/engine: progress, states, one per category, suggestion);
- `BudgetStore` (core/data);
- Settings → Budgets (list, form, "Use ₹X");
- the Home budgets card.

**Decisions applied:** a fresh limit each month, no rollover.

**Method:**
- Code analysis.
- Budget simulation `-Psim.engine.users=2000`: the database month view → `Budgets.progress`, against an independent sum
  over every entry. It covers several zones, custom month starts, splits, deleted entries, transfers and income, budgets
  merged from two phones and removed ones, and the suggestion against its definition.
- The full local gate is green.

**Status:** reported only. **Nothing fixed** (except SIM-000 below, found and fixed while building, test-first).

## Findings

1. **[FLAW-001] [Medium] Refunds don't come off a budget**
   - Where: `Budgets.progress` uses `spentByCategory`, which counts expense entries only. A refund is income, so
     it's never in a spending category (the form and merchant memory both keep it out).
   - How to reproduce: spend ₹4,500 on Food against a ₹5,000 budget (NEAR). Swiggy refunds ₹1,200. Food still shows
     ₹4,500.
   - Why it matters: refunds are common (cancelled orders, Amazon returns), and the budget reads as more used than it
     is, sometimes OVER when it isn't.
   - Direction (not a fix): "refunds linked to their originals" (plan B2). A refund linked to a Food payment counts
     against Food's spending. Until then, say "refunds aren't counted" on the budget form.

2. **[FLAW-002] [Medium] No alert when a budget passes 80 % or 100 %**
   - What happens: the warning exists only as the card's colour and text on Home. A capture that pushes Food over the
     limit says nothing.
   - Why it matters: most spending is captured in the background, so the user learns only when they open the app.
   - Direction: a local notification when a capture crosses 80 % or 100 %, once per budget per month. It needs the
     notification permission (Android 13+), and should hide amounts while the app lock is on, as the widget will.

3. **[FLAW-003] [Low] Changing a limit rewrites how past months look**
   - Where: one `Budget` per category with no history, and Home applies today's limit to any month shown.
   - How to reproduce: Food was ₹5,000 in October (OK at ₹4,800). In November it's lowered to ₹4,000, and October
     now shows OVER.
   - Direction: show budgets only on the current month, or keep the limit's history (effective from the month it was
     set). The second is a backup format change, so a `schemaVersion` bump (rule 8.7).

4. **[FLAW-004] [Low] The suggestion can be too low for a new category**
   - Where: `BudgetStore.suggestion` counts months since the user's first entry, not since the category was first
     used.
   - How to reproduce: a user since June adds "Pets" in October and spends ₹3,000 in it. The November suggestion is
     (0 + 0 + 3,000) / 3 = ₹1,000.
   - Direction: count from the first month with spending in that category.

5. **[FLAW-005] [Low] Spending in no budget is invisible next to the budgets**
   - What happens: all budgets can show OK while most of the month's spending has no category, or is in categories
     without a budget.
   - Direction: a last line on the card: "₹12,400 spent outside your budgets".

6. **[FLAW-006] [Low] The card grows with every budget**
   - What happens: 15 budgets put the month's entries a long scroll below. On a small screen even 3 do (seen in the UI
     tests).
   - Direction: show the 3 fullest, then "All 15 budgets ›".

7. **[FLAW-007] [Low] A transition month gets a full month's limit**
   - Where: when the user changes the month's start day, `MonthSchedule` makes a short or long transition month
     (e.g. 25 days). Its budget is still the full limit.
   - Direction: leave it, and say so on the form ("a changed start day makes one shorter month"); or prorate that one
     month.

8. **[Info] A deleted category's budget comes back with it**
   - The budget is hidden while its category is deleted. Re-adding the category name brings the category back (catalog
     #3), and with it the old budget, without saying so. Probably wanted; listed so it isn't a surprise.

9. **[Info] Things that held up**
   - Spending matches the independent sum for every budget-month in the simulation: splits by their own category,
     income and transfers never counted, entries in other zones in the right month (the shared month function).
   - Money stays in paise. The bar's length is the only fraction, drawn from the whole percent.
   - At exactly the limit it shows "₹0 left" and NEAR, not OVER.
   - A removed budget comes back with its id when set again. There's a limit of 10,000 like other records, and the
     backup format is unchanged (budgets were already in v3).
   - The suggestion reads at most 3 months, one at a time: 4 ms for a 10-year user.
