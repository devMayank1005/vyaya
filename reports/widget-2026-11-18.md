## Findings

1. **[FLAW-001] [Medium] "₹289 left for today" can still be showing the next day**
   - Where: the widget is redrawn after a capture, when the app is left, when the setting or the lock changes, and by
     the system's periodic update (`updatePeriodMillis` 30 min). The periodic update is only a request: Android skips
     it while the phone sleeps (Doze), and many phones' battery savers delay it for hours.
   - How to reproduce (by analysis): turn amounts on, leave the phone idle overnight, look at the widget at 7 am. The
     line can still say yesterday's "left for today".
   - Why it matters: the widget's one number is wrong in the morning, the time it's most likely to be looked at.
   - Direction (not a fix): redraw at the next local midnight with an inexact alarm (no extra permission), and on
     the date, time and time-zone change broadcasts. Or date the line ("Tue: ₹289 left").

2. **[SEC-001] [Medium] Amounts can stay on the widget after the app lock is turned on**
   - Where: turning the lock on calls `QuickAddWidget.refresh`. That redraw runs in the background, and its errors are
     swallowed.
   - How to reproduce (by analysis): with amounts shown, turn the app lock on, then swipe Vyaya away from recent apps
     at once. The process can die before the redraw lands, and the widget keeps showing the amount until the next
     periodic update (see #1: possibly hours).
   - Why it matters: the user turned on the lock to hide their money, and the home screen still shows it.
   - Direction: when the lock turns on, draw the hidden widget at once and synchronously (it needs no database read).
     Draw it hidden whenever an update can't be worked out, rather than leaving the last one.

3. **[PEN-001] [Low] Any app can make the widget redraw, as often as it likes**
   - Where: the widget receiver must be exported for the launcher, and `APPWIDGET_UPDATE` isn't a protected
     broadcast. `onUpdate` uses the widget ids the sender passes.
   - What happens: each broadcast reads the setting and, with amounts on, the month. A loop of broadcasts costs battery.
     No data goes back to the sender. Ids that aren't ours should be ignored by the system; to check on a device.
   - Direction: ignore the sender's ids and use `getAppWidgetIds` for this widget; redraw at most once every few
     seconds.

4. **[FLAW-002] [Low] Tapping the widget's Add while editing an entry drops the edit**
   - Where: an add request sets `editing = null; adding = true`.
   - How to reproduce: open an entry, change its amount, go to the home screen, tap Add on the widget. The edit form
     is replaced by an empty add form, and the change is lost without a word.
   - Direction: when a form is open, keep it (or ask "Discard your changes?").

5. **[FLAW-003] [Low] The widget's "Nothing safe to spend this month" doesn't say why**
   - Where: `WidgetText` has one line for every case with a daily amount of zero. Home says whether it's bills still
     due, what friends owe, or nothing left.
   - Direction: short variants, such as "₹0 today: bills still due" or "₹0 today: friends owe you ₹800".

6. **[SEC-002] [Low] Existing, found by lint: an Android 9 call on Android 8**
   - Where: `NotificationCapture.kt:48` calls `Person.getUri` (API 28). The app supports API 26 and up.
   - Analysis: it is reached only when a listed app's notification carries a people list on Android 8 / 8.1. There,
     the `Person` class doesn't exist, so reading the list should fail first, inside the `try` that drops the
     notification. A crash is unlikely but not proven. Lint reports it as an error, so lint can't be added to CI as
     it stands.
   - Direction: guard it with an SDK check, and add `lintPlayDebug` to the CI android job.

7. **[PEN-002] [Info] Any app can open Vyaya's add form**
   - Where: `ACTION_ADD` on the exported launcher activity.
   - What happens: the same as opening the app. No extras are read, so nothing can be filled in, and the app lock asks
     first.

8. **[Info] Things that held up**
   - Amounts are off by default. The setting is kept in the database's prefs table, which a backup never carries. It
     is disabled, and says why, while the app lock is on.
   - The widget is for the home screen only (`widgetCategory="home_screen"`), never the lock screen.
   - Add and the tile go through `MainActivity`, so the app lock asks first. A request is answered once, after
     unlocking. It isn't answered again at the next unlock or after a rotation.
   - The tile can be bound only by the system (`BIND_QUICK_SETTINGS_TILE`, manifest test). On a locked phone, the
     phone's own unlock comes first.
   - Both PendingIntents are explicit (to `MainActivity`) and immutable (test).
   - With amounts hidden, the widget reads only the setting, no money.
   - The widget and Home share one calculation: 0 differ over 8,000 simulated days.
   - iOS has no widget yet (WidgetKit is in Phase 4).
